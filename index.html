🚗 TN Vehicle SecurePay

Smart Vehicle, Licence & Fine Management System

Welcome to TN Vehicle SecurePay

This project prototype connects vehicle records, licence verification, identity verification, traffic fine management and demo Auto-Pay in one secure platform.

🧪 Demo Vehicle

Use this vehicle number for testing

TN 01 AB 1234

✨ Available Features

All project modules are shown here in one main website.

🚗

Vehicle Registration

Vehicle owner can register vehicle details in the system.

🪪

Licence Verification

Driving licence verification status is shown as a demo.

🆔

Identity Verification

Identity verification status is displayed with masked data.

💰

Fine Management

View fine amount, violation, due date and payment status.

🔔

Due-Date Reminder

Pending fine due dates and overdue warnings are displayed.

💳

3-Month Auto-Pay

Demo bank authorization supports three-month Auto-Pay.

📜

Payment History

Paid fine details, payment method and paid date are saved.

🧾

Digital Receipt

Paid fine can generate a printable digital payment receipt.

🚨

Stolen Vehicle Alert

Stolen vehicle status is prominently shown to the owner.

👮

Officer Verification

Transport officer can search and verify vehicle records.

📊

Officer Statistics

Total vehicle, fines, payment and stolen vehicle summary.

🔐

Masked Data Security

Aadhaar and bank account details are masked in this demo.

🚪 Access Project Modules

👤 Vehicle Owner

Register vehicle, check fine records, see reminders, pay through Demo Auto-Pay, view Payment History and generate Digital Receipt.

👮 Transport Officer

Search vehicle records, verify owner details and identify or flag stolen vehicles.

🔄 Application Flow

🚗 Vehicle Register vehicle number
🪪 Licence Demo verification
🆔 Identity Masked identity check
🏦 Bank Demo authorization
💳 Auto-Pay Fine payment demo
👮 Officer Vehicle verification

🔐 Privacy & Security Notice

This is a college project prototype. Aadhaar numbers, bank credentials and sensitive owner data are masked.

No real Aadhaar verification, bank linking or money transaction happens in this application.
